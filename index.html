FersmithAI: Personalized AI/ML Learning Platform Live

                        A learning platform for people who hold a full-time job and have 1-2 hours a day to move into AI/ML. Every morning it asks two questions, how many minutes you have and what you're working toward, then builds that day's plan to fit the answer.
                        

                        

                        Every skill comes with a podcast series (theory, real failures, interview prep), a video summary, a code example, a case study, flashcards and interview questions. The hands-on labs cover tools like Docker, Git, MLflow and LangGraph, each with its own case study, and there's a glossary and a set of paper breakdowns on top.
                        

                        

                        I built the media pipeline behind it in Python. It turns each draft episode into a transcript, labels the two speakers with Whisper and renders the final audio with Gemini TTS. Videos are assembled from slides and voice, with Whisper subtitles generated on a local GPU. The assessment scores each skill and reorders the learner's queue, so the daily plan starts with what they need most.
                        

                        

                        Most people stitch this together from three separate subscriptions: one for courses, one for practice, one for interview prep. Here it's one, and all of it is AI/ML. Built solo on Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage), Paddle for billing, and Vercel for hosting.

SkyAnalyst-AI: Cabin Operations Intelligence Framework

                        A modular NLP framework that transforms unstructured flight cabin reports into structured operational insights using RAG and abstractive summarization. Built on ChromaDB vector storage and all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings for semantic retrieval across 2,500+ synthetic incident reports, so search runs on meaning instead of keywords. Summarization is powered by DistilBART-CNN, delivering 4x faster inference than standard BART with negligible quality loss. Includes an exploratory analytics dashboard with sentiment intensity scoring and statistical visualizations across 5 incident categories and 22 flight routes.

Children’s Tale Summarizer: Master’s Thesis, NLP Pipeline & Flask App

                        At the time, Turkish had very few language models trained on creative writing, and the available ones (T5, BERT, GPT variants) pulled sentences out of a text instead of writing a real summary. For my master’s thesis I built an end-to-end pipeline that writes abstractive summaries of Turkish children’s tales and illustrates them.
                        

                        

                        I tested more than 15 transformer models on 13 tales, then tuned the generation settings of the top three (temperature, beam search, length limits, repetition penalty). That reached up to 2× ROUGE-L (0.25 to 0.52) and +3 points of BERTScore F1.
                        

                        

                        The Flask app takes a PDF or TXT file, summarizes the tale in three parts (introduction, development, conclusion), translates the summary into English and draws an illustration for each part with Stable Diffusion. Authors can get original illustrations for a book without drawing them, and 90% of users found the visuals matched the story.
